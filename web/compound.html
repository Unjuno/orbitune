Playback contract

Generation, decoding and sampled rendering remain separate components.

  • A2-512 generation runs locally through ONNX Runtime Web/WASM.
  • Decoding supports nucleus, top-k, min-p and hybrid filtering without modifying model weights.
  • GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 is rendered through SpessaSynth 4.3.14.
  • Model graphs and SoundFont bytes are independently SHA-256 verified.
  • Infinite generation and the event monitor both remain bounded in retained state.
