How this stream behaves

Important properties of the current runtime.

  • Generation and playback happen locally through ONNX Runtime Web/WASM.
  • The model carries fixed-size local, hierarchical, and recurrent state rather than the complete song history.
  • Changing Base/LoRA variants begins a fresh stream; recurrent state is not reused across variants.
  • Preview audio follows generated PROGRAM/BANK state, maps GM program families to distinct WebAudio timbres, and renders channel 10 as percussion. MIDI export preserves the exact symbolic output.
