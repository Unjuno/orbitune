Playback contract

The browser renderer no longer substitutes generated instrumentation with oscillator families.

  • A2-512 generation runs locally through ONNX Runtime Web/WASM.
  • GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 is a real sampled SF2 bank rendered through SpessaSynth 4.3.14.
  • Model graphs and SoundFont bytes are independently SHA-256 verified.
  • The SoundFont is served from Orbitune Pages rather than hotlinked from its upstream download.
  • Infinite generation retains bounded model state; sampled synth state resets on Stop.
